2. What Pairnets is

  • The software: the apps for Windows, Mac and Linux, and the nest (server) software.

You can use the software on your own domain without an account. Then the parts about the account don't apply to you.

3. The software licence

The software is licensed under the MIT licence. These terms don't take away anything it allows. Section 4 adds a few rules for the official builds we publish and update.

4. The official apps

We publish official builds of Pairnets: the Windows app and its installer (PairnetsSetup.exe), the Mac and Linux apps, and the nest (server) software installed with get.sh. They come from pairnets.app and from our releases on GitHub. The MIT licence applies to all Pairnets code, including our builds. This section only adds how our builds update, report crashes and use the name.

  • Updates. The apps look for new versions by themselves. You can turn this off in Settings (“Check for updates by itself”). When an update is ready, the app tells you, and it installs when you choose. Every update is checked against our release key before it is installed.
  • Your nest. The app updates your nest only when you agree, or when you have turned on “Update the nest by itself” (off unless you turn it on). A nest installed with get.sh is also updated when you run get.sh again.
  • What changes. An update can add, change or remove features, for example to fix bugs or keep Pairnets safe. What changed is in the release notes.
  • Crash and bug reports. The apps send a report only when you press Send. The Privacy Policy says what a report holds.
  • The name. MRnigth uses the name “Pairnets” and its logo for the official builds. The MIT licence covers the code, not the name. If you share a changed version, please give it a different name and logo, so nobody mistakes it for ours.
  • Other people's code. Pairnets includes open-source parts made by others, each under its own licence. They are on the open-source licences page.
  • Stopping. You can stop at any time by uninstalling Pairnets.

Besides the normal releases, you can choose a beta or dev channel to try new versions before everyone else. Choosing one means you accept this section.

  • They are for testing. They can have serious bugs, lose or damage files, or stop working. Use them only with files you have a backup of, and not on your only copy.
  • You can switch back to the normal channel at any time. You then get the next normal release.
  • What you see in a beta isn't final and may never be released. Please don't present it as the finished app.
  • Crash and bug reports work as in the normal apps: they are sent only when you press Send.

Everything else in these terms applies to beta and dev builds too, including “Free, and as it is” and “Liability”.

6. Your account

  • You must be at least 13 years old, or older if the law where you live requires it.
  • Use a real email address that you can read.
  • Keep your ways of signing in safe. Anyone who can sign in to your account can allow new computers to reach your nests.
  • You are responsible for what happens with your account. An account is for one person: don't share it.
  • An account can have up to 4 nests of its own.
  • You can let friends use your nests with their own accounts (up to 10 per nest). You decide who gets in and what they may do, and you are responsible for what you share with them.
  • You can link one of your nests with a friend's nest (up to 4 linked nests per nest), so the two nests keep shared folders in step. The same rules apply as for sharing with a friend.
  • An account with no sign-in or other activity for 1 year is deleted. We email you 30 days before, and signing in once keeps it.
  • If you think someone else got in, sign out everywhere on your account page and tell us at support@pairnets.app.

7. Your nest and your files

You own your files, and we claim no rights to them. Your nest runs on your own computer. You are responsible for it, for what's stored on it, for keeping it updated, and for anyone you let use it.

Pairnets keeps old versions of files for a while, but it is not a backup. Keep your own copy of anything important.

8. Fair use

Follow the Guidelines. In short: nothing illegal, don't attack or overload the service, and don't try to get into anything that isn't yours.

Traffic through the relay has a fair-use limit per nest, so that one nest can't slow the service down for everyone else.

9. The API

The API at sync.pairnets.app (addresses under /v1/, and the relay under /n/) is made for the Pairnets apps, nests and installer.

  • You may use it with your own tools for your own account, if you follow these terms and the Guidelines.
  • Stay within the limits, and slow down when the service asks you to (an answer of 429 with Retry-After).
  • Say who you are: send an honest User-Agent.
  • Don't resell access to the API, or offer it to others as a service.
  • The API can change or disappear at any time, without notice, except for what the current Pairnets apps need.

11. Changing or ending the service

We may change or stop the service. If we plan to shut down the account service, we'll tell you by email at least 30 days before, whenever we can. Your files are on your nest and stay there, and a nest can also be used on your own domain without the service.

You can stop using Pairnets at any time and delete your account.

We may suspend or close an account, or block a nest or computer from the service, if it breaks these terms or the Guidelines, puts other people or the service at risk, or if the law requires it. Where it's reasonable, we'll tell you first and explain why.

13. Illegal content and reports

This section is how Pairnets meets the EU's Digital Services Act.

  • What we hold. Your files are stored on your own nest, not by us: the service only passes them through. What the service keeps is your account: email address, username, nest and computer names, the names of folders you share, and invites (see the Privacy Policy).
  • Contact point. support@pairnets.app is the single point of contact for users and for authorities, including the European Commission, the European Board for Digital Services and the Danish authorities. Write in English or Danish.
  • Reporting illegal content or abuse. Email support@pairnets.app with “Abuse” in the subject. Say what it is and why you think it's illegal, where it is (a username, invite link or nest name), your name and email address (not needed when you report child sexual abuse material), and that you believe in good faith that your report is correct and complete. We confirm that we got it, look at it with care and without undue delay, and tell you what we decided and how to challenge it.
  • What we can do. We can't see or remove files on your nest. We act on the account, the nest's link to the service, or a computer: a warning, blocking a nest or computer from the service, or suspending or closing the account (see section 12 and the Guidelines). If we learn of anything that threatens someone's life or safety, we tell the police.
  • If we act on your account. We tell you what we did and why: the facts, the rule it broke, and how to challenge it, unless the law or someone's safety stops us. A person decides, never an automatic system. Reply to support@pairnets.app and a person looks at it again. You can also go to the courts or to an out-of-court dispute settlement body certified under the Digital Services Act.
