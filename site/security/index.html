Security

How Pairnets protects your files, and how to report a problem safely.

Last updated 10 October 2026

How Pairnets keeps your files safe

  • Your files stay on your own server. The service never stores them.
  • Every computer has its own key. Remove a lost computer on your nest, and its key stops working at once.
  • A new computer only gets in after you check its code and press Allow.
  • Keys, sign-in links and sessions are only stored in hashed form.
  • HTTPS everywhere, strict content security rules on every page, and cookies that only our own site can read.
  • Your nest opens no ports: it connects out to Cloudflare through a tunnel.
  • Old versions of files are kept for 30 days, so a bad change can be undone.

What it doesn't protect against

  • There's no end-to-end encryption yet. Cloudflare, where the service runs, can see traffic in transit.
  • Anyone who can sign in to your Pairnets account, and the service itself, can allow a new computer to reach your nest. Protect your email and Google account.
  • A computer that someone has taken over can read and change your synced files.

All the details are in the security design.

Report a security problem

Email support@pairnets.app with “Security” in the subject. Please don't open a public issue on GitHub.

  • Say what you found, the steps to see it, and what someone could do with it.
  • We aim to reply within 7 days, keep you updated, and fix serious problems as fast as we can.
  • Please give us 90 days, or until a fix is out, before you tell others.
  • Once it's fixed, we're happy to thank you by name on this page, if you'd like. Pairnets is a personal project, so there's no bug bounty.

Rules for testing

  • Only test with your own account, your own nest and your own computers.
  • Don't look at, change or delete other people's data. If you come across any, stop and tell us.
  • No denial of service, flooding or spam. Stay within the limits.
  • No phishing or tricking people, and no physical attacks.

If you follow these rules in good faith, we see your research as allowed and won't take legal action against you.

Not covered

  • Problems in Cloudflare, Google, GitHub or Resend themselves: please report those to them.
  • Reports from automated scanners that show no real way to cause harm.
  • Missing best-practice settings that can't be used to cause harm.
  • Attacks that need a computer that has already been taken over.

This page is also listed in security.txt.

pairnets.app only serves these pages. Your Pairnets account lives at sync.pairnets.app, which connects your computers to your nest. Your files are stored only on your nest: they pass through sync.pairnets.app on the way and are never kept there. Only computers you have allowed can get in.
