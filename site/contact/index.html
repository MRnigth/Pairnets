Found a bug?

Open an issue on GitHub, or email us. Issues are public, so never paste keys, tokens, sign-in links, IP addresses or file contents.

In the app, the ⋯ menu → Report a bug… builds a report you can copy into the issue or an email. Nothing is sent by itself.

Security problem?

Please don't post it publicly. The Security page explains how to report it privately.

Privacy request?

Email us from the address on your account, with “Privacy request” in the subject. We answer within one month. To delete everything yourself, see Delete my account.

Who runs Pairnets

Pairnets is made and run by MRnigth, a private person in Denmark. It is a free, non-commercial project: there's no company, office or phone line, so email is the way to reach us.

  • Email: support@pairnets.app, in English or Danish. We usually answer within a few days.
  • Contact point for authorities and users under the EU Digital Services Act: the same address. See Terms, section 11.
  • Abuse or illegal content: “Abuse” in the subject. Privacy: “Privacy request”. Security: “Security”.
  • Name and address: MRnigth is the name Pairnets' maker uses in public, and we don't publish a home address. Authorities, and anyone who needs them for a legal claim or a request about their data, can get our full name and postal address by email.

pairnets.app only serves these pages. Your Pairnets account lives at sync.pairnets.app, which connects your computers to your nest. Your files are stored only on your nest: they pass through sync.pairnets.app on the way and are never kept there. Only computers allowed on your account can get in.
