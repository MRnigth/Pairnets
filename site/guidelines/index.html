Guidelines

Pairnets is for keeping your own files in sync. These few rules keep it working for everyone.

Last updated 10 October 2026

Do

  • Sync files that you have the right to keep.
  • Keep your account, your nest and your computers secure.
  • Link only nests and computers that are yours, or that you're allowed to use.
  • Tell us about bugs, and report security problems privately (see Security).

Don't: illegal or harmful content

  • No illegal content. Child sexual abuse material is never tolerated: we close the account and report it to the police.
  • No malware, or files meant to harm other people's computers.
  • No material that you have no right to share, if you share your nest with others.

Your files live on your own server and we don't look at them, so you are responsible for what you keep there. We act on reports, and on what we can see of how the service is used.

Don't: misuse the service or the API

  • Don't overload it. Stay within the limits (300 requests a minute from one address), and slow down when the service asks you to.
  • Don't get around the bot check, the limits or the sign-in.
  • Don't try to get into accounts, nests or computers that aren't yours.
  • Don't scan or attack the service, except for security research under the rules on the Security page.
  • Don't make accounts in bulk or with scripts, and don't use the service to send spam.
  • Don't resell access to the service.

Don't: pretend

  • Don't pretend to be Pairnets or someone else.
  • Don't use the Pairnets name to mislead people, for example on a fake sign-in page.

Your nest, your rules

If you let other people use your nest, you set the rules for them, and these Guidelines apply to them too.

If the rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is, we may give a warning, block a computer or nest from the service, suspend or delete the account, or report it to the police. We tell you why, unless the law or someone's safety stops us. If you think we got it wrong, reply to us at support@pairnets.app.

Report a problem

Email support@pairnets.app with “Abuse” in the subject. Say what happened and where. For security problems, see the Security page.

These Guidelines are part of the Terms of Service.

pairnets.app only serves these pages. Your Pairnets account lives at sync.pairnets.app, which connects your computers to your nest. Your files are stored only on your nest: they pass through sync.pairnets.app on the way and are never kept there. Only computers you have allowed can get in.
