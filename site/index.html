One folder, the same on all your computers.

Pairnets keeps a folder identical on your computers through a small server you own. Your files are never stored in someone else's cloud.

Pairnets is for computers for now. A phone app will use the same sign-in.

Three steps

No server addresses or secret tokens to copy.

  1. Get the app

    Install Pairnets on the computer. It lives in your tray or menu bar and keeps one folder in sync.

  2. Sign in with your browser

    Choose Continue with email (or Continue with Google) in the app. Your browser opens sync.pairnets.app; you check that the code matches and press Allow.

  3. Pick a folder

    It catches up with your other computers, then stays in sync. Switch computers any time.

The server is a small program on a machine you own. Only computers you have approved get in.

It's reached through a Cloudflare Tunnel, so there are no ports to open. This website never sees your files.

Never overwrites silently

If two computers change the same file, both versions are kept. Every overwritten or deleted file stays on your server for 30 days.

One key per computer

Lost a laptop? Remove just that computer from your nest and its key stops working at once. Nothing else changes.

It lives in your tray or menu bar

Pairnets keeps working when its window is closed. The tray shows what is moving, and lets you pause.

Your nest

Your own server: any always-on Ubuntu computer. Run one command, then open the link it shows and add the nest to your Pairnets account. No domain, no Cloudflare account.

curl -fsSL https://pairnets.app/get.sh | sudo bash

The guide has the details, and a way to use your own domain instead.
