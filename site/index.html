One folder, the same on all your computers.

Pairnets keeps a folder identical on your computers through a small server you own. Your files are never stored in someone else's cloud.

Set up in three steps

From download to in sync

No server addresses or secret tokens to copy. You sign in with your browser and pick a folder.

  1. 1Get the app

    Install Pairnets

    It lives in your tray or menu bar and keeps one folder in sync.

  2. 2Sign in

    Sign in with your browser

    Choose Continue with email (or Continue with Google) in the app. Your browser opens sync.pairnets.app; check that the code matches and press Allow.

  3. 3Pick a folder

    Choose what to keep in sync

    It catches up with your other computers, then stays in sync. Switch computers any time.

Safety

Built to keep your files safe

Yours, not ours
The server is a small program on a machine you own, reached through a Cloudflare Tunnel, so there are no ports to open. Only computers you have approved get in. This website never sees your files.
Never overwrites silently
If two computers change the same file, both versions are kept. Every overwritten or deleted file stays on your server for 30 days.
One key per computer
Lost a laptop? Remove just that computer from your nest and its key stops working at once. Nothing else changes.
