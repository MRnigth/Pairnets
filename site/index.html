Pairnets is for computers for now. A phone app will use the same sign-in.

Three steps

1

Get the app

Install Pairnets on the computer. It lives in your tray or menu bar and keeps one folder in sync.

2

Sign in with your browser

Type your nest's name and press Sign in. Your browser opens your nest; you check a code and press Allow. No server addresses or secret tokens to copy.

3

Pick a folder

It catches up with your other computers, then stays in sync. Switch computers any time.

Built to keep your files safe

Yours, not ours

The server is a small program on a machine you own, reached through a Cloudflare Tunnel — no ports to open. Only computers you have approved get in. This website never sees your files.

Never overwrites silently

If two computers change the same file, both versions are kept. Every overwritten or deleted file stays on your server for 30 days.

One key per computer

Lost a laptop? Remove just that computer from your nest and its key stops working at once. Nothing else changes.

Your nest (your own server)

Any always-on Ubuntu computer. Run one command, then open the link it shows and add the nest to your Pairnets account. No domain, no Cloudflare account.

curl -fsSL https://pairnets.app/get.sh | sudo bash

The guide has the details, and a way to use your own domain instead.

pairnets.app only serves these pages. Your Pairnets account lives at sync.pairnets.app, which connects your computers to your nest. Your files are stored only on your nest: they pass through sync.pairnets.app on the way and are never kept there. Only computers you have allowed can get in.
