Cookie settings

Pairnets only uses the cookies it needs to sign you in. There's nothing to switch off, so the cookie notice just tells you that.

Last updated 10 October 2026

Your settings

Necessary

Keep you signed in to your Pairnets account and keep signing in safe. Pairnets can't work without them.

Analytics

Pairnets doesn't use any analytics or statistics cookies.

Advertising

Pairnets has no ads, and no advertising or tracking cookies.

Because Pairnets only uses necessary cookies, the law doesn't ask us to get your consent, and there's nothing to choose. The cookie notice at the bottom of our pages just tells you this, and remembers that you closed it. If we ever want optional cookies, we'll ask you first, and these switches will work.

The cookies we use

pairnets.app (this website)

None. This website sets no cookies.

When you close the cookie notice, it keeps one small note in your browser's local storage, pn-cookie-notice, so the notice doesn't come back on every page. That's all it's for. It isn't a cookie, and it's never sent anywhere.

sync.pairnets.app (your account)

CookieWhat it's forHow long
__Host-pn_idKeeps you signed in to your account30 days after you last use it, 90 days at most. Removed when you sign out.
__Host-pn_gKeeps a Google sign-in safe while you're on Google's page10 minutes
__Host-pn_elTies an email sign-in link to the browser that asked for it15 minutes

Only sync.pairnets.app can read them, and only over HTTPS: not scripts on the page, and not other websites.

Like this website, it also keeps the one small pn-cookie-notice note in your browser's local storage, only to remember that you closed the cookie notice. It isn't a cookie, and it's never sent anywhere.

Bot check. On the sign-in page, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you're a person, so bots can't send sign-in emails. Cloudflare runs it under its own privacy policy and doesn't use it for ads.

Your nest

If you sign in to your nest's own website (a nest on your own domain), the nest sets __Host-pn_session to keep you signed in, for 30 days after you last use it. It's your server, so it's your cookie.

The apps

The apps don't use cookies. They keep their settings on your computer.

Removing cookies

Sign out on your account page to remove the sign-in cookie, or clear the site data for sync.pairnets.app in your browser's settings. You'll just need to sign in again.

pairnets.app only serves these pages. Your Pairnets account lives at sync.pairnets.app, which connects your computers to your nest. Your files are stored only on your nest: they pass through sync.pairnets.app on the way and are never kept there. Only computers you have allowed can get in.
