Add a computer

Three steps, on the computer you want to add. No server address or token to type.

  1. Install Pairnets

    WindowsMacLinux

    Intel Mac? Use this one.

  2. Sign in with your account

    Open Pairnets and choose Continue with email (or Continue with Google), with the same account your nest belongs to. Your browser opens sync.pairnets.app, where you check that the code matches and press Allow. Nothing to type about your nest, no token to copy.

    Your nest runs on its own domain? Choose "I run my own nest on my own domain" at the bottom of the window instead.

  3. Pick a folder

    Choose the folder to keep in sync. It catches up with your other computers, then stays in sync on its own.

Pairnets says your nest is not connected? Check that the computer your nest runs on is switched on and online. Here's what to check.

No nest yet? On an always-on Ubuntu computer, run curl -fsSL https://pairnets.app/get.sh | sudo bash and open the link it shows. The guide has the details.

pairnets.app only serves these pages. Your Pairnets account lives at sync.pairnets.app, which connects your computers to your nest. Your files are stored only on your nest: they pass through sync.pairnets.app on the way and are never kept there. Only computers you have allowed can get in.
