Help

Quick answers for signing in and reaching your nest. Start here. General questions? See the FAQ.

Can't open nest.pairnets.app?

Your nest is your own server, so the name only works while that machine — and its tunnel to the internet — is running.

  1. Check the name is typed exactly: nest.pairnets.app.
  2. Reload the page, or try from another device. Works there? Then it's this device's internet connection.
  3. On the server: sudo systemctl status pairnets-server pairnets-tunnel — both should say active (running).

Still stuck? The guide has a "Check it from outside" section with more.

I just installed the server: how do I get in?

At the end, the installer prints a one-time setup link for your nest. Open it in your browser: it works once, within 24 hours. Then add a passkey, or a password of at least 10 characters. After that, sign in at your nest's own name, for example https://nest.pairnets.app.

Lost the link, or it expired? On the server, make a new one and open it:

sudo -u pairnets /opt/pairnets/pairnets-server owner-link

Now add your computers: Add a computer.

"Can't reach it" under "Your nest" in the app

Same causes: the name is mistyped, or your nest isn't running. Fix that, then change the name slightly and back, or press "Sign in with your browser" again; the line under the box checks the address as you type.

The code expired, or my nest said no

Codes last 10 minutes. Press Get a new code (or Start over) in the app and approve the new one. Always check that the code on your nest matches the code in the app before pressing Allow.

"Signed out of your nest"

This computer was removed on your nest, or you pressed Sign out of this computer. Nothing is lost: your files are untouched. Press Sign in again… and approve it on your nest.

I lost my passkey or password

On the server, make a new one-time sign-in link and open it in your browser:

sudo -u pairnets /opt/pairnets/pairnets-server owner-link
A computer was lost or stolen

Open your nest, go to Devices, and remove it from the ⋯ menu. Its key stops working at once and nothing else changes.

Which of my data does pairnets.app see?

None. This website is static pages with no accounts and no scripts that talk to anyone. Your nest is your own server; signing in and approving computers happens there, never here.

More

The full guide covers installing the server, how syncing and conflicts work, restoring old versions, and updates. General questions, like what Pairnets costs or where your files go, are in the FAQ. Still stuck? Get in touch.

pairnets.app only serves these pages. Your Pairnets account lives at sync.pairnets.app, which connects your computers to your nest. Your files are stored only on your nest: they pass through sync.pairnets.app on the way and are never kept there. Only computers you have allowed can get in.
