Start here

My nest doesn't show up, or the app says it isn't connected

Your nest is your own server, so it only answers while that machine, and its link out to the internet, is running.

  1. Check that the computer your nest runs on is switched on and online.
  2. Sign in at sync.pairnets.app/account with the account your nest belongs to. It should be listed there, and say whether it is online.
  3. On the server, run this. Both should say active (running). 
    sudo systemctl status pairnets-server pairnets-tunnel
  4. Not listed at all? Run the one-line installer again and open the link it shows: 
    curl -fsSL https://pairnets.app/get.sh | sudo bash

Still stuck? The guide has a "Check it from outside" section with more.

I just installed the server: how do I add it to my account?#

At the end, the installer prints a link. Open it in your browser (on any device), sign in with the email address or Google account you want the nest to belong to, check that the page shows the same code as the server, and press Add this nest. The link works for 15 minutes; if it runs out, run the installer command again.

Your nest then appears at sync.pairnets.app/account. Now add your computers: Add a computer.

"Can't reach it" in the app#

Your nest is switched off or offline, or it was removed from your account. Work through the checklist at the top of this page. If your nest runs on its own domain, check that the address is typed exactly, then press "Sign in with your browser" again; the line under the box checks the address as you type.

The code expired, or the sign-in said no#

Codes last 10 minutes. Press Get a new code (or Start over) in the app and approve the new one. Always check that the code on the sign-in page matches the code in the app before pressing Allow.

"Signed out" in the app#

This computer was removed from your account, or you pressed Sign out of this computer. Nothing is lost: your files are untouched. Press Sign in again… and allow the computer at sync.pairnets.app.

I can't sign in to my account#

Your Pairnets account has no password. Sign in with the same method as before: Continue with email (a link sent to your address) or Continue with Google. Check your spam folder, and that you use the same address as last time. The link works for 15 minutes.

A computer was lost or stolen#

Sign in at sync.pairnets.app/account and remove it there. Its key stops working at once and nothing else changes. If you run your nest on your own domain, remove it under Devices on the nest's own website instead.

My nest runs on its own domain and I lost my passkey or password#

This applies only to a nest with a website of its own. On the server, make a new one-time sign-in link and open it in your browser:

sudo -u pairnets /opt/pairnets/pairnets-server owner-link

Which of my data does pairnets.app see?#

None of your files, and no account details. The pages here are static. The only thing they do is ask sync.pairnets.app, on every page, whether you are signed in, so the top bar can show your username instead of "Sign in". Your browser sends its sync.pairnets.app sign-in cookie with that question; pairnets.app never sees the cookie and keeps nothing. Cloudflare, which delivers the site, handles visitors' IP addresses. Details: Privacy.
