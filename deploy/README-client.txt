Pairnets for Windows
====================

Pairnets keeps one folder the same on all your computers, through your own
server: your "nest".

Getting started
1. Put Pairnets.exe in a permanent place, for example
   %LocalAppData%\Programs\Pairnets\Pairnets.exe
2. Start Pairnets.
3. Under "Your nest", type your nest's name, for example sync.example.com
4. Press "Sign in with your browser".
   (If your nest offers them, "Continue with Google" or "Continue with email"
   work too.)
5. Your browser opens your nest's page. Sign in there, check that it shows
   the same code as Pairnets, and approve this computer.
6. Back in Pairnets, choose the folder to keep in sync and press
   "Start syncing".

Pairnets then runs in the notification area, next to the clock. To start it
with Windows, tick "Start Pairnets when I sign in to Windows" in step 6, or
right-click its icon and tick "Start with Windows".

To start again from the beginning: right-click the icon, choose
"Settings...", go to "Start over" and press "Reset this app...". This signs
this computer out of your nest and forgets its settings. The files in your
sync folder are never deleted.

Logs:     %LocalAppData%\Pairnets\logs
Settings: %AppData%\Pairnets\settings.json
Help:     https://pairnets.app/help
